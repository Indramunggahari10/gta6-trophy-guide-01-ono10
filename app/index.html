Before you start

  • Keep manual saves. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus. Save at the start of every chapter and once before the final mission.
  • One trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude needs Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1. Do it before Chapter 1 ends — and keep a Chapter 1 save until the trophy pops, since no source has confirmed the trophy strictly needs that one spot.
  • One race is time-sensitive: Race Against Yago in Chapter 3 (Yago’s street racing strand) shows an hourglass icon — do it the moment it appears. It feeds Strangers & Freaks, not its own trophy.
  • Cheats switch trophies off — and saving too. Keep cheats away from your trophy save.
  • Everything is single-player. All 55 trophies unlock offline; the 100% checklist appears under Progress > Stats after the story, but earlier work still counts.

Roadmap to the platinum

This order is this guide’s suggestion, built from the official trophy descriptions and the cited guides — the game itself publishes no official roadmap. Story first, then collectibles, then the open-world cleanup, then 100% completion, and the platinum pops last.
